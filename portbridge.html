01 / The opportunity

Consolidating wealth
starts with confidence.

The business hypothesis: customers may bring more of their existing investments to a platform when the transfer feels understandable and dependable. The proposal focuses on that moment of trust rather than a broad redesign of investing.

CUSTOMER NEED

“What is happening to my shares?”

A customer needs visibility during a handoff between institutions, with clear instructions if progress depends on them.

BUSINESS GOAL

Earn more of the existing portfolio.

Increase successfully completed inbound assets while reducing avoidable transfer enquiries and abandoned attempts.

PRODUCT RESPONSE

A transfer hub with a next step.

Guided initiation, evidence-backed milestones and a focused route to resolve missing information.

02 / Discovery & approach

From broad friction
to one focused problem.

Discovery brought together public investor discussions, customer reviews and consumer-finance resources. I used the resulting themes to frame a product hypothesis, map a specific journey and choose an MVP.

01 / GATHER SIGNALS

Look for recurring questions.

The discovery synthesis drew on Finanztip, Trustpilot, NeueBanken, Depotkonto and Wertpapier-Forum. Themes included uncertain status, manual steps and acquisition-data concerns.

02 / MAP THE JOURNEY

Locate the uncertain handoffs.

Follow the customer from intent to initiation, sending-broker action, securities arrival and data review. Separate waiting from information the customer can act on.

03 / CHOOSE A PROBLEM

Prioritise trust at transfer time.

Compare user consequences, relevance to asset consolidation and the scope a focused product can influence. Use qualitative prioritisation to select a coherent first release.

04 / MAKE IT TANGIBLE

Prototype the critical moments.

Build the transfer wizard, milestone view and missing-document path so the decisions can be explored rather than described only in a document.

The central insight: waiting is harder when the customer cannot tell whether the process is moving or needs their attention.

Research scope: secondary research provides directional signals rather than a representative view of all customers. The case study makes no claim of primary interviews or measured product outcomes.

03 / The person behind the transfer

Meet Max Mustermann.

A fictional scenario persona that makes the proposed journey concrete. Max is a customer in the case study and not the author or an interviewed participant.

Max Mustermann

Berlin · Senior Product Manager at Zalando
Fictional customer persona

Employee equity + personal investing

Holds vested employee shares in an external equity-plan account and wants a clearer view of his investments in one place.

The job to be done

When I move vested employee shares, I want to understand the transfer and resolve missing information so I can consolidate my portfolio with confidence.

What matters

Clear progress, consistent records and knowing whether he needs to act.

Where confidence breaks

Long periods without updates and uncertainty about acquisition information after shares arrive.

The current workaround

Check both providers, revisit instructions and ask support for an update.

Design implication

Show the last confirmed event, the responsible party and a specific next step.

04 / Priorities & product decisions

Choose the problem.
Then constrain the solution.

The original exploration also raised support exceptions, cash separation, tax-management complexity and household accounts. Inbound transfers became the focus because they connect a concrete trust problem to the asset-consolidation hypothesis.

01

Guided initiation before direct integrations.

A custodian-aware wizard makes the requested information and next action clear. Direct integrations remain a possible later option rather than a dependency for demonstrating the experience.

Tradeoff: a guided flow cannot remove the sending institution’s own process or guarantee that a request has been accepted.

02

Confirmed milestones before an animated promise.

Make the last known status, update time and responsible party visible. Only advance a live service when a trusted event or operations confirmation supports the change.

Tradeoff: some periods remain uncertain. “Awaiting confirmation” is more useful than invented real-time precision.

03

Acquisition-data review as a separate concern.

Securities and their related data may progress separately. Show missing information without implying that an uploaded statement automatically establishes an accepted tax basis.

Tradeoff: document extraction can assist a reviewer, but exceptions and cross-border differences still need an accountable review path.

04

Explain the customer action, not the settlement protocol.

Translate proposed internal events into plain-language messages. Keep technical records available to operations without making the customer interpret clearing messages.

Tradeoff: the simplified journey needs careful mapping to actual operational states before implementation.

Every status should

Prepare: understand eligibility and the sending broker’s requirements. Wait: see the last confirmation and responsible party. Resolve: provide requested documents and track their review.
MVP focus
  • Custodian-aware preparation and a reference summary
  • Milestone history with last confirmed status
  • Missing-information request and review status

06 / From interface to delivery

The experience depends
on reliable handoffs.

A proposed implementation would need an internal transfer record, a trusted source for each status and an operations route for exceptions. The prototype demonstrates the interaction without claiming access to Trade Republic’s architecture.

RECORD

One transfer reference.

Associate requested holdings, the sending institution and customer actions with a consistent case record. Check eligibility and ownership before a real request proceeds.

INTERPRET

Evidence becomes a status.

Normalize partner messages or reviewed updates, handle duplicates and preserve event history. Never treat a silent system as proof of progress.

RESOLVE

Keep exceptions visible.

Route missing or inconsistent data to review. Show a clear customer request and record who resolved it rather than claiming a document upload completes reconciliation.

Industry reference: Upvest’s documentation describes initiation, counterparty notification, settlement and subsequent tax-data transfer as distinct stages. It informs this conceptual separation and does not establish Trade Republic’s implementation. Read the reference ↗

07 / Success measures

Measure completion.
Protect correctness.

The primary measure is the value of successfully completed inbound assets per quarter. Requested assets and completed assets should be reported separately so an unfinished pipeline cannot be mistaken for delivered value.

Transfer completionProposed target · above 85%

Share of eligible initiated transfers that reach completion within a defined observation window. Confirm the baseline and eligibility rules before using the target.

Time to completionProposed target · below 10 days

Time from accepted initiation to the defined completion state. Report securities arrival and acquisition-data review separately. The original 18-day comparison is not treated as an established baseline.

Transfer-related enquiriesProposed target · 30% lower

Compare support contacts per initiated transfer against a measured baseline. A lower contact rate only counts as improvement if customers can still reach support and resolve problems.

Trust & operational guardrailsNo numerical outcome claimed

Track unresolved data discrepancies, misleading status reports, complaints, exception workload and security incidents alongside completion. Monitor the effect on compliance review quality rather than rewarding speed alone.

All numerical targets are proposals from the original project brief. They are not achieved results or verified company benchmarks.

Full project narrative

Context. An independent wealth-product proposal exploring how a clearer transfer experience could support customers consolidating existing investments.

Discovery. Start with secondary-research signals, separate user complaints from prevalence evidence and map the customer’s handoffs between institutions. A fictional employee-equity persona narrows the scenario.

Prioritisation. Choose inbound-transfer clarity over a collection of unrelated banking features. The rationale is a direct connection between the customer’s uncertainty and the proposed business goal of completed asset inflows.

Solution. Guide preparation, display trusted milestones and make missing information actionable. Avoid making external APIs or automated tax reconciliation prerequisites for demonstrating the product value.

Delivery considerations. Connect the interface to a reference-based case record, confirmed operational events and an accountable review process. A production service would require partner, eligibility, security and data-handling design beyond this prototype.

Measurement. Evaluate completed asset inflows, transfer completion, time to completion and support contacts. Treat correctness, exception burden and customer trust as guardrails. The numerical goals remain proposed targets.

Product learning. A useful financial interface makes the limits of its knowledge visible. The product should distinguish waiting from an actionable exception and shares arriving from related records being reviewed.
